## Action Items for Next Sprint
1. **Code Reviews First** – Prioritize short code-review sessions (10–15 mins) throughout the week to ensure quality.
2. **Finalize pipeline and testing** – Complete final stages of CI/CD setup, ESLint, and unit testing coverage.
3. **Page Compatibility** – Ensure site works across major browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari) and devices.
4. **In-file Cleanup** – Remove unused variables, comments, and unused imports.
5. **FIGMA Alignment** – Make sure final UI matches locked Figma designs.
6. **Local Storage Features** – Finish implementation across pages like records and results.
7. **Implement Leaderboard Logic** – Finalize backend logic for leaderboard display and rendering.
8. **Frontend Design QA** – Clean up and test layout/styling across all core pages (matching, sequence, homepage, results).
9. **Lock Sequence Difficulty Flow** – Default to Easy mode and prevent empty state on page load.
10. **Document with JSDoc** – All JS functions must have clear documentation before merge.
11. **GitHub Issue** – All new work should have labeled and typed issues; clean up stale or incomplete tasks.
